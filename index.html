To make this perfect for a phone, we need to handle a few mobile-specific issues: Zooming: On mobile, when the "Yes" button grows, it can trigger the browser to zoom in or push buttons off-screen. Touch Delays: Some mobile browsers have a slight delay on clicks. Button Overlap: As "Yes" grows, it might cover the "No" button entirely, making it impossible for her to keep clicking "No" (which is the fun part!). Here is the refined, mobile-optimized version.
